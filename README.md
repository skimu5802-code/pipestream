# 🌟 Key Features

## 🎧 Background Play & Audio Mode

* Keep the music playing while using other apps or with your screen locked.
* Switch to **audio-only streaming** to save mobile data and conserve battery life.

## 📥 High-Quality Offline Downloads

* Download videos in multiple resolutions:

  * **1080p**
  * **720p**
  * **480p**
  * **360p**
* Download videos directly as standalone audio tracks in **M4A/MP3** format.
* Full-featured download manager with support to **pause, resume, and manage** your offline media library.

## 🖼️ Picture-in-Picture (PiP) & Mini-Player

* Multitask effortlessly with floating **PiP window** support.
* Dockable bottom **mini-player** lets you browse playlists and trending videos while your stream continues.

## 🔗 Instant Link Detection & Share Sheet Support

### Clipboard Detection

Automatically detects copied YouTube links and presents quick options to **Watch** or **Download**.

### Direct Sharing

Share any video directly from the **YouTube app, web browser, or messaging apps** to open it instantly in the player.

## 🔍 Smart Search & Discovery

* Real-time search suggestions and quick search history.
* Explore curated **Trending** feeds across:

  * 🎵 Music
  * 🎮 Gaming
  * 📰 News
  * And more
* View channel profiles and video comments.
* Jump directly to **chapter timestamps**.

## 🎛️ Cinematic Player Controls

* Fluid gesture controls for:

  * ☀️ Brightness
  * 🔊 Volume
  * ⏩ Fast seek scrubbing
* Variable playback speed from **0.25x to 2.0x**.
* **Repeat/Loop** playback modes.
* Full-screen immersive viewing experience.

## 🔒 Privacy First & Local Storage

* **No Google/YouTube account login required.**
* **Zero tracking** or invasive ads.
* Your bookmarks, history, and playlists are stored **100% locally on your device**.

# 📱 Technical Highlights

* Built with **Jetpack Compose** and **Material Design 3** for a modern, fluid, OLED-friendly dark theme.
* Powered by **AndroidX Media3 (ExoPlayer)**.
* **Failover stream resolution** ensures smooth, low-latency playback.
